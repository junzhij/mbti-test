How it works

For each statement, pick how much you agree. Answer as you usually are, not as you'd like to be. There are no right answers.

The test scores you on four dimensions:

  • E / I — Extraversion vs. Introversion: where you get your energy
  • S / N — Sensing vs. Intuition: how you take in information
  • T / F — Thinking vs. Feeling: how you make decisions
  • J / P — Judging vs. Perceiving: how you approach structure
